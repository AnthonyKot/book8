Still Working

Seven short histories. Each starts with something ordinary and follows it back to where it was made. Read one in ten minutes, or listen.

  1. 1 Opium Wars Britain fought a war to keep buying Chinese tea, then built a supply so it would not have to. Read, or listen · 8 min
  2. 2 The War on Somebody Else’s Ground A 1904 battle on Chinese ground ended in steel that is valuable today because it has never seen a bomb. Read, or listen · 16 min
  3. 3 The Cold War’s First Sentence The iron curtain was a theatre’s fire safety before it was a border. It still comes down before every performance. Read, or listen · 8 min
  4. 4 The Scramble for Africa The bark that let Europeans go inland is what makes tonic water bitter. Under a black light it glows. Read, or listen · 15 min
  5. 5 Who started 1914 In 1914 the border read cargo, not people. The airport queue is the table that replaced it, still being read. Read, or listen · 13 min
  6. 6 1917 On the night the Winter Palace was taken, the trams kept running. The picture everyone carries was staged twice, years later. Read, or listen · 16 min
  7. 7 Korea A permanent member walked out of the Security Council. Its empty chair let the Council authorise a force, and the rule it learned still governs the room. Read, or listen · 9 min
