About

Method

This book compares how school (and a few near-school) history textbooks narrate the same modern events. It is not a survey of what “really happened.” It is a survey of classrooms.

Rules

  • Quotes or nothing — no claim about a textbook without a passage, edition, and page.
  • Machine translation is a map — English research extracts locate pages; chapter quotes from non-English books use the original line plus a reading translation.
  • Absence is graded — a missing topic is avoidance only when most in-scope books cover it; shared silence is curriculum norm.
  • No contempt register — the coda asks the reader to check their own book, which fails if the prose has already taught them to sneer.

Form

Each episode chapter runs a fixed rhythm: lede, one question, boring facts, a ladder of tellings (agentless → agentive), a short grammatical “tell,” credit where due, limits, receipts, and the same coda every time.
