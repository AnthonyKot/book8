# Chapter 5 Pitches: Versailles, 1919

## Candidate 1: The Submarine Cables of Yap (Material Hinge)
- **Seed Quote / Silence**: US and CN note that Shandong and German Pacific islands went to Japan; UK lists reparations. None mention that the very first physical asset confiscated in 1914 and formally reassigned at Versailles was Germany's 28,000-mile global undersea telegraph cable network.
- **The Loop**: Germany built an independent cable network to bypass British telegraph hubs → Britain cut Germany's Atlantic cables on day two of WWI → Versailles confiscated all German cables, awarding the vital Pacific junction at Yap to Japan → Japanese control of trans-Pacific telegraph communications triggered the 1921 US–Japan crisis at Washington.
- **Shared Hook**: The fiber-optic cables carrying modern international internet traffic lie in the exact seabed trenches charted, severed, and carved up at Versailles in 1919.

## Candidate 2: The Mosul Oil Tap & The 25% Share (Material Hinge)
- **Seed Quote / Silence**: SD notes that the Paris Peace Conference led to San Remo and "a new link in the chain of colonialism" via mandates; UK and US treat reparations purely as German cash.
- **The Loop**: Before 1914, Deutsche Bank held a 25% stake in the Turkish Petroleum Company's Mosul oil rights → Versailles confiscated Germany’s 25% share as reparations → At San Remo (1920), Britain transferred that 25% oil share to France in exchange for French acquiescence to a British mandate over Mosul → France founded CFP (now TotalEnergies) on that 25% German share while Britain secured Iraqi oil.
- **Shared Hook**: Drivers refueling at TotalEnergies or BP stations today are using oil infrastructure whose corporate shares were traded in Paris to reconcile European reparations with Middle Eastern mandates.

## Candidate 3: "Mandate" — The Roman Word That Rebranded Colonialism (Linguistic Hinge)
- **Seed Quote / Silence**: SD calls the mandate "a system devised by shrewd English politicians such as Lord Smuts," while RU labels it "the division of colonial booty."
- **The Loop**: Woodrow Wilson demanded "no annexations," so Jan Smuts repurposed a Roman legal term (*mandatum*: a trust administered without pay) → Article 22 of the Versailles Covenant declared colonial governance "a sacred trust of civilization" → Defeated territories were divided into A, B, and C mandates matching pre-war Allied imperial ambitions → The UN renamed them "Trust Territories" in 1945, cementing 1919 mandate boundaries as modern national borders (Syria, Lebanon, Iraq, Rwanda, Tanzania).
- **Shared Hook**: The passports and national borders of six modern sovereign states exist because a Roman legal term was revived at Versailles in 1919 to give colonial annexation a moral vocabulary.

## Candidate 4: Seized Patents & Synthetic Bread (Material Hinge)
- **Seed Quote / Silence**: UK lists £6,600 million and US lists "$30 billion" in financial reparations, but both ignore the largest transfer of industrial capability: German chemical patents.
- **The Loop**: Germany’s Haber-Bosch process fixed atmospheric nitrogen for synthetic fertilizer and WWI explosives → Annex IV of the Treaty of Versailles forced Germany to surrender all chemical patents and manufacturing secrets to Allied monopolies → Allied nations created modern domestic fertilizer industries (France's ONIA, Britain's ICI) from seized German formulas → German chemical firms merged into IG Farben (1925) to survive the loss of their global patent monopoly.
- **Shared Hook**: Synthetic nitrogen fertilizer — which sustains nearly half of the human population alive today — spread globally because its secret formulas were seized as physical reparations at Versailles in 1919.
