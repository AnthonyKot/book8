## The two quotations kept

1. **RU · Чубарьян 2023** — «Десятки тысяч польских военнослужащих были интернированы в этих странах, а также взяты в плен. Большинство из них было отпущено по домам, а некоторые, из наиболее антисоветски настроенных, расстреляны.» / “Tens of thousands of Polish servicemen were interned in these countries, as well as taken prisoner. Most of them were released home, and some, of the most anti-Soviet minded, shot.”
2. **DE · bpb 2012** — «Mehr als 381 000 Menschen wurden zwischen Februar 1940 und Juni 1941 aus dem sowjetisch besetzten Ostpolen nach Sibirien und Zentralasien deportiert, das polnische Offizierskorps, das sich vor dem deutschen Angriff in die Sowjetunion zu retten geglaubt hatte, verhaftet. 15 000 polnische Offiziere wurden im April und Mai 1940 von der sowjetischen Geheimpolizei erschossen, darunter rund 4500 im Wald von Katyn…» / “More than 381,000 people were deported from Soviet-occupied eastern Poland to Siberia and Central Asia between February 1940 and June 1941, and the Polish officer corps, which had believed itself to have escaped to the Soviet Union from the German attack, was arrested. 15,000 Polish officers were shot in April and May 1940 by the Soviet secret police, including around 4500 in the Katyn forest…”

Both quotations and translations match the former chapter text character for character; country and edition are identified with each quotation.

## Facts deliberately not added

- I was tempted to add the identities and occupations of individual victims, but the old page did not name any.
- I did not add details about how the killings were carried out beyond the old page’s description of the bodies and execution sites.
- I did not add the political or diplomatic circumstances around the 1943 announcement, the duration of the denial as a calculated number, or later memorial history.
