## The two quotations kept

1. **KZ · История Казахстана, 11 кл. (2015), extract p. 220 — the Karaul appeal reproduced in the textbook:** «За 40 лет здесь взорвались тысячи Хиросим.» / “Over 40 years, thousands of Hiroshimas have exploded here.”
2. **IT · Sabbatucci–Vidotto 2004, Il mondo contemporaneo (Laterza 2004), extract ~p. 493:** «La decisione di Truman serviva innanzitutto ad abbreviare una guerra che si annunciava ancora lunga e sanguinosa, ma aveva anche lo scopo di offrire al mondo (e soprattutto agli alleati rivali sovietici) la dimostrazione della potenza militare americana.» / “Truman’s decision served first of all to shorten a war that still promised to be long and bloody, but it also had the purpose of offering to the world (and above all to the rival Soviet allies) the demonstration of American military power.”

Both quotations, translations and displayed citations match the former chapter text character for character; the country and edition are identified immediately before each quotation.
