### Candidate 1: The Cotton-Salary Loop (Anticolonial Spark on the Nile)

- **The Causal Loop**: Wall Street stock crash → US/European textile demand drops → Sudanese Gezira cotton prices collapse → colonial budget deficit → British authorities impose selective salary cuts on African graduates while sparing European staff → Gordon College students strike and organize → birth of Sudan’s modern nationalist movement.
- **Shared Lander**: The cotton shirt in the reader's closet (or Khartoum University today).

### Candidate 2: The Protectionist Spiral (Smoot-Hawley & World Trade Collapse)
- **Seed from comparison**: OpenStax (US) and Sabbatucci–Vidotto (IT) highlight the Smoot-Hawley Tariff Act and America's failure to act as a "hegemonic power," while CN and IN note the global dominoes.
- **The Causal Loop**: US Congress passes Smoot-Hawley Tariff (1930) to shield domestic farmers → 60 countries retaliate with retaliatory tariffs → global trade value collapses by 66% → export-dependent nations (Germany, Japan) face economic devastation → militarist aggression (Manchuria 1931) and Nazi election gains (1933).
- **Shared Lander**: The term "Smoot-Hawley" invoked by modern news anchors and politicians whenever tariff wars break out.
- **Who tells it**: 2 of 8 books (US and IT explicitly name protectionist failure; UK mentions tariffs; RU/BY/CN/IN/SD skip the tariff retaliation mechanism).

### Candidate 3 (Non-obvious candidate): The Gold Standard Handcuffs (Monetary Deflation & Soviet Immunity)
- **Seed from comparison**: Belarus (BY) asks why the crisis "did not affect the economy of the Soviet state," while Italy (IT) notes Britain abandoning gold convertibility in September 1931.
- **The Causal Loop**: Post-WWI Gold Standard forces central banks to raise interest rates during capital flight → money supply contracts worldwide, locking in severe deflation → countries holding onto gold (US, France) suffer prolonged depression, while nations un-pegging early (UK, Japan 1931) recover → Soviet Union, non-convertible and outside the gold pool, buys discounted Western factory equipment during its First Five-Year Plan.
- **Shared Lander**: The phrase "gold standard" used daily as a metaphor for ultimate quality, or gold bullion in central bank vaults.
- **Who tells it**: 2 of 8 books (IT notes 1931 sterling unpegging; BY flags Soviet exception; 0 books explain the monetary gold transmission loop).

### Candidate 4: The Nitrogen-Surplus Loop (Synthetic Fertilizer & Pre-Crash Agricultural Ruin)
- **Seed from comparison**: Russia (RU) notes agricultural overstocking starting in 1928, and Lowe (UK) notes falling farm prices prior to Wall Street.
- **The Causal Loop**: Haber-Bosch synthetic nitrogen factories (built for WWI explosives) pivot to cheap farm fertilizer in the 1920s → global grain yields surge → agricultural prices collapse by 1927–1928 before Wall Street crashes → bankrupt farmers over-plow marginal prairie soil → Dust Bowl ecological disaster and mass displacement.
- **Shared Lander**: Synthetic fertilizer bags in home garden stores today, or modern bread prices.
- **Who tells it**: 0 of 8 books (all textbooks begin with Wall Street stock tickers; none explain how chemical nitrogen synthesis broke agricultural prices before the stock crash).
