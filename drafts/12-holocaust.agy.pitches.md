### Candidate 1: The Euphemistic Paper Trail — Pest Control to Industrial Extermination (Material & Linguistic Lander)

* **Seed from comparison**: Germany’s federal agency booklet (*bpb Nr. 316*) stresses that the Holocaust was coordinated across civilian state bureaucracy and ministries at Wannsee using administrative euphemisms like *“Vernichtung der Juden”* and *“Endlösung”*, while US, UK, and Italian textbooks name the gas chambers (*Gaskammern*) of Auschwitz and Majdanek.
* **The Causal Loop**: Industrial mass gassing did not begin with a bespoke weapon of war, but with a commercial agricultural pesticide. Zyklon B (hydrogen cyanide pellets) was developed by *Degesch* (*Deutsche Gesellschaft für Schädlingsbekämpfung*) for fumigating grain elevators and cargo ships. When the SS scaled up extermination at Auschwitz-Birkenau, they purchased metric tons of Zyklon B through standard commercial purchase orders and shipping invoices, declaring them to customs and railway authorities as "disinfection supplies against typhus." The SS ordered the manufacturer to remove the chemical odorant warning agent (normally added to alert humans to leaks), converting an agricultural pest-control product into an odorless killing agent while keeping the transaction inside routine corporate accounting.
* **The Shared Lander**: The word "pest control" or chemical fumigation warnings in modern agricultural and maritime supply chains, or the chemical odorants mandated in commercial natural gas today — a direct reminder of how routine commercial logistics and sanitary engineering were turned into industrialized murder.
* **Corpus Silence / Count**: Only 1 of 11 classrooms (OpenStax, US) mentions chemical extermination methods in technical context, and 0 of 11 explain how commercial chemical supply chains and routine corporate invoicing enabled the logistics of mass gassing.

### Candidate 2: The Dehomag Hollerith Code — Punch Cards and the Automation of Death (Technological Lander)

* **Seed from comparison**: The comparison highlights the sudden administrative leap from localized shootings (Einsatzgruppen) to continent-wide deportations: US OpenStax notes "collaboration of tens of thousands of people from across Europe", Britain’s Lowe cites Wannsee logistics to transport 11 million Jews, and Germany’s *bpb* notes that over eleven million were targeted across every country in Europe.
* **The Causal Loop**: Sorting, identifying, and deporting millions of people across a continent under wartime conditions required an administrative technology that index cards could not handle. The SS and Reich Ministry of the Interior relied on automated electromechanical punch-card tabulators designed by Herman Hollerith and manufactured by IBM’s German subsidiary, *Dehomag* (Deutsche Hollerith-Maschinen Gesellschaft). Punch cards coded every individual by national census data: Hole 8 indicated Jewish origin, Hole 2 indicated Warsaw, Hole 5 indicated execution or transport to Auschwitz. Tabulating machines were stationed at regional railroad hubs and concentration camp administrative offices, allowing a small cadre of bureaucrats to track trains, inventory human cargo, and optimize deportation logistics in real time.
* **The Shared Lander**: The precursor to modern relational databases and digital census tracking. The punch-card format pioneered for modern computing was the exact medium through which European census data was converted into deportation lists.
* **Corpus Silence / Count**: 0 of 11 classrooms explain the administrative technology or data-processing systems that made the continental scale of Nazi logistics possible.

### Candidate 3: Extermination Through Work — IG Farben and Corporate Balance-Sheet Slavery (Material Lander)

* **Seed from comparison**: Russia’s textbook labels concentration camps as *"death factories"* (*фабрики смерти*), Italy’s textbook notes that deportees were *"exploited to the point of physical exhaustion"*, and US OpenStax cites state policy to *"eliminate European Jewish people by working them to death"*.
* **The Causal Loop**: Extermination camps were not only military or ideological sites; they were commercial joint ventures. Auschwitz III (Monowitz) was constructed by IG Farben, Europe’s largest chemical conglomerate, with an investment of over 700 million Reichsmarks to manufacture synthetic rubber (*Buna*) and synthetic fuel. IG Farben corporate accountants established an explicit life-expectancy formula for slave laborers (averaging 3 to 4 months) and calculated the daily profit per prisoner after deducting the daily rental fee paid to the SS and minimal food rations. When prisoners became too weak to work, corporate managers turned them over to the SS for gassing at Birkenau and requested fresh consignments — transforming human destruction into a line-item asset on corporate balance sheets.
* **The Shared Lander**: Synthetic rubber in modern vehicle tires or the corporate successor companies of IG Farben (such as Bayer and BASF) that remain major global chemical manufacturers today.
* **Corpus Silence / Count**: 2 of 11 classrooms (RU, IT) use the phrase "factories of death" or "physical exhaustion," but 0 of 11 detail how private corporate balance sheets and industrial conglomerates financed and profited from extermination through labor.

### Candidate 4: Babi Yar and the Erasure of Topography (Geographic Lander — Non-Obvious Candidate)

* **Seed from comparison**: The comparison surfaces a stark numerical and nominative fight at Babi Yar: Russia’s textbook records *"about 70 thousand Jews were shot"* (folding the ravine's multi-year total into a single entry), while German and British textbooks record *"more than 33,000"* or *"some 34,000"* Jews shot in two days (29–30 September 1941). Meanwhile, Soviet history historically subsumed Jewish victims under generic "Soviet citizens," and Ukraine's textbook mentions 6 million dead only as background to Israel's 1948 founding.
* **The Causal Loop**: Babi Yar was a physical geographical feature—a deep ravine on the outskirts of Kyiv. In September 1941, SS Sonderkommando 4a, Police Battalions, and Wehrmacht units marched Kyiv’s Jewish population to the ravine, forced them to hand over valuables and clothes, and shot them into the pit. In 1943, as the Red Army advanced, the SS launched *Aktion 1005*: chain-ganged prisoners were forced to exhume tens of thousands of corpses, burn them on giant bonfires made of railway ties, and crush the bones with granite tombstones to erase physical evidence. In the postwar era, Soviet authorities poured industrial mud into the ravine, built a highway, a park, and a television tower over the site, refusing for decades to erect a monument that named Jewish victims. The physical landscape of murder was systematically altered twice: first by the perpetrators to destroy evidence, then by urban planners to erase specific memory.
* **The Shared Lander**: A ravine, city park, or broadcast tower in modern Kyiv—a concrete urban landscape where physical geography, mass murder, and memory politics collide.
* **Corpus Silence / Count**: 3 of 11 classrooms (RU, DE, UK) name Babi Yar, but 0 of 11 explain *Aktion 1005*, the physical exhumation campaign to erase evidence, or the postwar urban engineering that covered the ravine.
