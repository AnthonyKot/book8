## Pitch 1 — Babi Yar’s two windows

**Seed:** RU «около 70 тыс. евреев» *were shot* at Babi Yar (p. 171) vs DE «mehr als 33 000 Menschen» shot by SS and police / UK «some 34 000 Jews» in two days (DE p. 29, UK p. 177) — same ravine, numbers that do not match on the page.

**Loop:** The lower figure is the two-day massacre of 29–30 September 1941 (Einsatzgruppen report 33,771); the higher figure is the order of magnitude often attached to the site across the longer occupation. Classrooms that compress both windows into one number without saying so produce a false agreement or a false fight.

**Lander (shared):** Babi Yar is still a place on the map of Kyiv and a memorial people can stand in; the number printed under a student’s finger is a counting-window choice, not a vibe.

**Who tells the gap:** 0 of 11 explain the spread; 3 put any number on the ravine (RU, DE, UK).

## Pitch 2 — Wannsee as a street address

**Seed:** DE alone stages the Wannsee Villa on 20 January 1942 — «Vertreter von Ministerien und NS-Bürokratie» coordinate the «Vernichtung der Juden» (p. 42); US and UK name the conference as logistics/state policy; RU/UA/IT never need the house.

**Loop:** Mass murder here is not only a Führer order and not only a camp — it is ministries and bureaucrats agreeing how to deport and kill, under a euphemism (“Final Solution”) that still has to be taught as vocabulary because ordinary words failed on purpose.

**Lander (shared):** The Wannsee Conference House is a museum at Am Großen Wannsee 56–58 in Berlin; the protocol is a document a reader can open online in minutes. <!-- CHECK: Haus der Wannsee-Konferenz memorial/museum operating at that address -->

**Who tells any of the house-as-system:** 3 of 11 (DE, UK, US) name Wannsee; 1 (DE) puts multi-agency coordination in the grammar of self-implication.

## Pitch 3 — “Soviet people” / “Soviet citizens” as a second ledger

**Seed:** KZ «планомерному истреблению советского народа» (p. 201); RU stacks Holocaust ~6 million Jews + camp dead ~11 million (Jews, Russians, Ukrainians, Poles…) + «советских граждан… 13,7 млн» on occupied territory (pp. 193–194, 202); BY never names Jews as murder victims at all.

**Loop:** A true multi-million Soviet civilian catastrophe and a specifically Jewish genocide share the same years and often the same ground; classrooms that have only one noun (“Soviet people”) or that absorb Jews into a multi-national camp total after naming them are not inventing deaths — they are choosing which deaths the sentence is for.

**Lander (shared, linguistic):** The word **genocide** entered English and international law in the mid-1940s (Raphael Lemkin; UN Genocide Convention 1948) so that the destruction of a people could not be filed only as “war losses” or “occupation victims.” <!-- CHECK: Lemkin, Axis Rule in Occupied Europe (1944); Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide, 9 Dec 1948 --> Readers in every corpus country still use the word in news; few school books show what category-fight it was built to settle.

**Who tells the dual ledger problem:** RU alone runs both Jewish and Soviet-citizen tallies in one volume; KZ alone (national genre) uses only “Soviet people”; BY/IN run neither Jewish murder narrative.

## Pitch 4 — Einsatzgruppen: the unit the German booklet will not let you forget *(non-obvious / own)*

**Seed:** US names «Einsatzgruppen (“operational groups”)» and prints Ohlendorf’s Nuremberg line that Jews and commissars were to be liquidated (pp. 544–545); DE builds the mobile killing phase with Einsatzgruppen, named HSSPF actions, and Wehrmacht complicity (pp. 28–29); RU’s Babi Yar sentence has Jews and no unit; IT jumps from Hitler’s project to SS camps; UA has only “the Nazis.”

**Loop:** Before the gas chambers became the emblem, the murder of Jews in the East was often a shooting pit behind the front — police, SS, army logistics, local auxiliaries. A classroom that goes straight to Auschwitz without the mobile phase teaches a true camp story and still misses the first industrial year of killing.

**Lander (shared):** Nuremberg Einsatzgruppen trial testimony (including Ohlendorf) remains in public archives and classroom video clips; the word itself is German because the units were. <!-- CHECK: US v. Ohlendorf et al. (Einsatzgruppen trial), 1947–48, Nuremberg Military Tribunal II -->

**Who names the units:** 2 of 11 (DE, US) use Einsatzgruppen; UK says “SS units and the regular army” without the term; RU/IT/UA do not put a unit noun on the early shootings.
